**🎯 Graham Weaver – Designing a Winnable Game (Summary & Lessons)**

---

### 🔥 Introduction

- Speaker: Graham Weaver – private equity founder, inspirational speaker.
    
- Theme: Redefining success by playing the _right_ game, not just playing _better_.
    

---

### 💣 Rock Bottom Moment – October 2008

- Lehman Brothers collapsed; market down 40%.
    
- Largest investor withdrew; predicted all others would too.
    
- Weaver realized he had only **12 months of savings** to survive.
    
- Emotional breakdown: newborn daughter depending on him, no solution in sight.
    
- Realization: He was asking the wrong question → Not “how to win this game?” but **“Am I playing the right game?”**
    

---

### 🎮 Shift: Redefining a Winnable Game

- **Winnable Game Definition**:
    
    - A game you _can_ win.
        
    - A game you _want_ to win.
        
    - A game that makes you more of who you want to become.
        
    - Where _external success_ and _internal fulfillment_ align.
        

---

### 🪄 Steps to Designing a Winnable Game

#### 1. **Choose a Game That Stirs Your Blood**

- Story: College rowing tryouts → didn’t make the cut → became a "land warrior".
    
- Trained obsessively every day alone.
    
- Met Mike Teti (Olympic rower/coach), who said: _“If you keep this up, you'll make the Olympic team.”_
    
- Lesson: **High-energy goals activate identity transformation.**
    
- **Daniel Burnham quote**: "Make no little plans for they have no power to stir men's blood."
    

#### 2. **Assume the Identity of Your Goal**

- Wrote daily: _“I am an Olympian”_ in notebook + 3 action steps.
    
- Began to _believe_ he was already an Olympian.
    
- Took daily action aligned with that identity.
    
- Despite setbacks (losing seat races, not making boats), grew consistently.
    
- Senior year: varsity team, captain, won nationals, top 2k meter time in U.S.
    

#### 3. **Don’t Lower the Bar – Set Bigger Goals**

- Alpine Fund’s initial goal: _“Generate attractive risk-adjusted returns while preserving capital.”_
    
    - Equivalent of aiming to make third-string.
        
    - Identity assumed: fearful, defensive, limited.
        
- Better question: _“What would you do if you knew you wouldn’t fail?”_
    
    - Took months to answer due to limiting beliefs.
        
    - New goal: **#1 private equity fund in the world**; **5x return on every fund**.
        
    - Reality at the time: vastly underqualified → just like his rowing start.
        
    - Result: Shifted energy, identity, team, investments → hit 5x on 3 of 4 funds (4th on track).
        

#### 4. **High Goals = Higher Probability of Success**

- **Counterintuitive truth**:
    
    - Bigger goals → greater energy, clarity, resilience.
        
    - You show up differently: focused, bold, long-term.
        
    - Attracts better people, actions, and results.
        
- **Setting moderate goals = more likely to fail.**
    

---

### 🧭 Final Lessons & Key Takeaways

- **Have a North Star**: A purpose that pulls you out of bed daily.
    
- **Don’t settle** – aim for something that **scares and excites you**.
    
- **Success is not manifestation without work** – it’s aligning identity + consistent action with a bold vision.
    
- **“What would you do if you knew you wouldn’t fail?”** – revisit this monthly.
    
- You don’t need to play _better_—you need to play the _right_ game.

### 🔥 **Steps to Designing a Winnable Game**

---

#### **1. Choose a Game That Stirs Your Blood**

- Select a goal that excites you deeply.
    
- You have a better chance of achieving an inspiring goal than a “reasonable” one.
    
- Ask: _“What would I do if I knew I wouldn’t fail?”_
    
- Let that guide your vision.
    

---

#### **2. Design Your Own Game**

- Don't follow the crowded, conventional path.
    
- Most admired people (authors, entrepreneurs, etc.) designed their own game.
    
- Conventional “rules” are often just habits — not actual requirements.
    
- In private equity, they boiled the game down to just 3 real rules:
    
    1. Return more money to investors than they gave.
        
    2. Act ethically.
        
    3. Stay aligned with values.
        
- Ask disruptive questions to find your game:
    
    - What do customers _hate_ about this industry?
        
    - What are competitors _unwilling_ to do?
        
    - What _problem breaks your heart_?
        
    - What _assumptions_ are you making?
        
    - What do you _believe_ that few others agree with?
        
- Scale your **bright spots**:
    
    - Focus on what’s working instead of what’s broken.
        
    - Their best deals came from backing people from their own team.
        
- Find intersections:
    
    - Where the world needs something **+** what gives you energy = **your edge/purpose**.
        
- Creativity is the work — **build the capabilities** to play your game.
    

---

#### **3. Play with People You Admire**

- People shape your goals, values, and identity.
    
- Don’t stay in toxic cultures (e.g., Larry story on Wall Street).
    
- Choose people who:
    
    - Share your values.
        
    - Celebrate your life outside of work.
        
    - Care about you as a person.
        
- Avoid environments that pressure you to abandon what matters.
    
- Your favorite memories will involve people you care about.
    

---

#### **4. Play Now**

- Most dangerous words: **“Not now.”**
    
- Life doesn’t start _after_ business school, promotion, job change, etc.
    
- Those things you’re “getting through” **are** your life.
    
- Passion comes from being passionate about your current life.
    
- Stop hedging or sitting on the sidelines.
    
- You don’t need to start a company to design your game — just start.
    
- You’ll find your winnable game by **doing**, not waiting.
    

---

### 🧭 Key Principles to Remember:

- **Winnable games aren’t found — they’re designed.**
    
- **Most rules are just conventional wisdom — ignore them.**
    
- **Look for energy + unmet need = where you’ll thrive.**
    
- **Play the long game with people who lift you up.**
    
- **Act now. You are enough. Start today.**
    

---

### 🏁 Final Questions to Guide You:

- What would you do if you knew you wouldn’t fail?
    
- What game will you play with your one precious life?
    
- Who will you play it with?
    
- Why wait?
    

**Play big. Play now.**
