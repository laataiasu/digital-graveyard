## Managing for Results: Key Takeaways

As a manager, it's essential to focus on pleasing others at all costs initially, but eventually, prioritize your own values and interests to achieve true success.

### Building Trust and Respect

* **Respect is key**: Prioritize respect over love when starting out as a manager. Respect builds trust, which is crucial for long-term relationships.
* **Trust is hard to build and easy to destroy**: Treat trust as your most precious asset, as it can be easily broken but rarely fully repaired.
* **Integrity and character are essential**: Develop a strong sense of integrity and character to help you navigate difficult situations and make tough decisions.

### Effective Leadership

* **Emphasize results over personal fulfillment**: Focus on producing results that achieve your goals, rather than just doing what's good for others.
* **Set priorities and manage time effectively**: Establish clear priorities, learn to balance work and personal life, and avoid burnout by making efficient use of your time (e.g., learning to say no).
* **Lead from a place of strength, not weakness**: Recognize when you're struggling with certain topics or feelings. Accept that it's okay to ask for help and be willing to learn alongside others.

### Conflict and Change

* **Be tough-minded but open-minded**: Approach conflicts as opportunities for growth rather than threats to your ego.
* **Manage changes carefully**: Make informed decisions about how changes can affect you, your team, and stakeholders. Prepare for potential consequences and develop clear communication strategies.
* **Compromise when necessary**: Develop an ability to find common ground and work towards mutually beneficial solutions.

### Finding Power and Clarity

* **Balance is critical**: Strive for a healthy balance between different aspects of life. When you're out of balance, it can affect your relationships and decision-making.
* **Communicate clearly**: Focus on expressing yourself assertively in order to ensure that you're heard accurately.
* **Power comes from within**: Don't feel powerless because someone else has authority over you; instead, take responsibility for managing power.

### Prioritizing Values

* **Understand your organization's values**: Identify and align with business objectives. Use these objectives as guidelines when making decisions to avoid compromise of integrity or essential aspects.
* **Value alignment drives success**: Make sure everyone involved has their personal value-driven vision in clear sight, which often involves understanding what truly matters for success.

### Growth and Learning

* **Forgiveness is a journey**: Keep working towards forgiveness and learn how this journey helps people grow. There's usually too much to invest to allow someone untrustworthy to remain in the team for long.
* **Self-awareness is key**: Be willing to understand that your own thoughts and feelings are going to go out of balance at some point since you can't be present somewhere at every moment of time.
