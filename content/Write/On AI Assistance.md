# On AI Assistance

Most essays here began as entries in my private journal — raw, unfiltered, sometimes written in two languages at once. Some of those entries were later drafted into essay form with AI assistance: the thoughts, experiences, and conclusions are mine; the polish is shared.

I'd rather write that down than pretend every paragraph emerged perfectly formed. If a piece reads smoother than the rest of this garden, that's why.
