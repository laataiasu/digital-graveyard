When I was little, I had extra skin near my ear, like a wart. It wasn't a disease, but it created a difference between me and others. Some friends would always ask what it was on my ear. I always felt uncomfortable about it, even though no one ever mocked me for it; it was just that I was uncomfortable with it. 

My mom suggested that I should have it removed surgically if it bothered me. At first, I was scared, but eventually, I became brave enough to go through with the surgery. As an adult, I've come to realize that it's silly to worry and overthink about being different from others, and that there is nothing wrong with it. I am trying to understand that everyone has their own insecurities.
