GF's story from her graphic design internship at [[Kompas Gramedia]]: Kompas wants to launch some new program, and it needs approval from the police. To get that approval they have to pay them — cash handed over "under the book" (off the record). She told me this casually; I honestly don't know whether it counts as corruption or not. It smells like it — payment to officials for approval, unrecorded cash — but it's so normalized here that nobody in the room probably blinks.
