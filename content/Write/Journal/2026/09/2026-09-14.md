cleaned up workstation desktop environment today.

officially stopping using hyprland. the config churn (hyprlang .conf deprecation vs unstable .lua transitions) and needing to stitch together separate daemons (`waybar`, `mako`, `cliphist`, `hyprpaper`) just wasn't worth the maintenance headache. 

backed up all old hyprland configs and scripts into `~/.archive/hyprland-backup-20260914_213036.tar.gz` and pruned all packages (`hyprland`, `waybar`, `mako`, `cliphist`, `hyprpaper`, `hyprvalidate`).

moving 100% to **niri** paired with **dms** (dankmaterialshell). infinite horizontal scrollable ribbon is so much better than binary space partition squishing windows, and dms handles bar, control center, notifications, clipboard, and wallpapers natively. 

also keeping stock **gnome wayland** installed out-of-the-box as a safety net in case niri or graphics drivers ever glitch.
