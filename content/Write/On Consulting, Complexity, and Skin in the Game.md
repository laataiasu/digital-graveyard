on consulting

when i was an intern at traveloka, i thougt data warehouse engineer is the most boring job, and beside you can't go anywhere because the world there is just e-commerce. so i tried to find consulting job where you can switch project periodically. then i got a first job after graduate at telkomsel's vendor, which i think worse than traveloka. i resigned for just 8 month, because it's so boring. and then i did nothing a few months until i get another vendor/consulting job again at accenture. oh god, actually there's more boring work. there's like a training of 1 month, its just repeating general material of IT development lifecycle, its really wasting my time. then i got another project, as data quality operation, god its so boring too. they got product, with lots of bugs, because the design from the beginning is just bad, so my main work is just fixing bugs other develops which is so useless because if the design was good, there will be no work for me. i think that's the essential of consulting. 

consulting on one hand became someone to blame, thats why government or state owned enterprise often use consulting work, so they can find someone to blame. many consultants said they are underpaid, some are overpaid. but with the burst of AI, it's of course they are overpaid. 

so the main point is, consultant exist to be pushed and to be blamed. and the irony of consultant is, if you provide a best solution of your work, you will run out of project. thats why most consultant make their work too complex. so that nobody understand it, so people depends on them. a best consultant solve the problem fastly and eleganty, that people would not pay them much because their work would become simple and understandable easily. and most client are stupid, so they tend to use something that is complex, as long as they got a guarantee or stupid license. thats why, for me become a consultant is like an unethical liar. if you do good job and they could understand what you're working on, they won't continue your contract, because they can do the work by themselves. thats why most consultant is struggling to get project in the age of AI, because now lots of information become free. 

i once heard from steve jobs too, he's belittle of consultant, they got no skin in the game he said. because what's consultant is doing will not that deep, they did the work only because they want the cashflow. its different when you build your own company, you do the work because you want to survive, and to generate profit. while consultant won't think that deeply, they just do the work based on billed contract. 

some said that if you're fresh graduate, you should join consultant because its very dynamic and you will learn a lot of things. yes, i am kind of agree with it, if you're lost and don't know what to do, just join consultant. i think generalist are people of don't have conviction, don't have the gut to bet on something, so they just want to deal with something temporary, they are afraid to commit to something. 

and another thing about my company which the nature is consulting is, your quality of life depends on the culture of the client and your team, the industry, etc. perhaps you can make friends easily in office with your generation that has different project. i think thats one that i like about my company, there's a batch, so you're not hired alone, so you can get to know with others, and the bond is just good even your friends have different project. so even if you get unlucky project (manager or client or industry), you will at least have someone to talk to when lunch, or someone to invite to fill your weekend. 

if you got high income from doing consulting job, then im happy for you, enjoy while it last. because if i am given opportunity again to go back to consulting, i am afraid i want to do it again, 2-3 years is enough. i need to do real job. i need to do meaningful and impactful job, bismillah, aamiin.
