Here’s a **lazy-friendly list** -- all are **cheap, easy to find in Semarang**, and **can be eaten raw or after very quick prep (steam / boil)**.

---

## 🥬 **Vegetables (Raw / Lightly Boiled OK)**

### 1. 🥕 **Carrot (Wortel)**

- **Eat raw:** slice thin, or grate into salad.
    
- **Quick cook:** steam 2–3 min until slightly soft.
    
- **Nutrients:** beta-carotene (vitamin A), helps brighten skin, good for eyes.
    
- **Cheap tip:** buy in pasar, lasts a week in fridge.
    

---

### 2. 🥬 **Spinach (Bayam)**

- **Eat raw:** baby bayam leaves are fine in salad.
    
- **Quick cook:** boil 1 min in hot water, strain immediately.
    
- **Nutrients:** iron, folate, vitamin C -- helps blood circulation and healthy glow.
    
- **Tip:** cook only what you’ll eat (wilts fast).
    

---

### 3. 🌿 **Cucumber (Timun)**

- **Eat raw:** slice or dice, sprinkle a little salt or lime juice.
    
- **Nutrients:** hydration, silica (for smooth skin).
    
- **Tip:** store in fridge; very cheap year-round.
    

---

### 4. 🍅 **Tomato (Tomat)**

- **Eat raw:** slice, sprinkle salt or olive oil (optional).
    
- **Quick cook:** 30 sec in hot water boosts lycopene absorption.
    
- **Nutrients:** lycopene, vitamin C -- protects from sun damage, keeps skin soft.
    
- **Tip:** small local tomatoes (“tomat sayur”) are cheaper and sweeter.
    

---

### 5. 🥒 **Long Beans (Kacang Panjang)**

- **Eat raw:** traditionally in _lalapan_ (Indonesian raw vegetable plate).
    
- **Quick cook:** blanch 1–2 min only.
    
- **Nutrients:** vitamin A, fiber, plant protein.
    
- **Tip:** pair with sambal or peanut sauce (like gado-gado).
    

---

### 6. 🥔 **Sweet Potato (Ubi Jalar)**

- Not raw (hard to digest), but…
    
- **Quick cook:** steam or boil 10 min, eat plain or with a little salt.
    
- **Nutrients:** beta-carotene, fiber, good carbs.
    
- **Tip:** keep a few steamed ones in fridge -- easy snack anytime.
    

---

## 🍎 **Fruits (Best Raw, Just Wash & Eat)**

### 1. 🍌 **Banana (Pisang Raja / Kepok / Ambon)**

- Ready to eat, no cooking needed.
    
- **Nutrients:** potassium, B-vitamins for energy and smooth skin.
    
- **Tip:** local varieties are cheaper and more flavorful.
    

---

### 2. 🍉 **Watermelon (Semangka)**

- **Eat raw:** chilled slices = hydrating & refreshing.
    
- **Nutrients:** lycopene, vitamin A, vitamin C.
    
- **Tip:** keep pre-cut pieces in fridge for lazy snacking.
    

---

### 3. 🥭 **Mango (Mangga)**

- **Eat raw:** slice ripe mango, eat directly.
    
- **Nutrients:** beta-carotene, vitamin C -- helps collagen and glow.
    
- **Tip:** when in season, super cheap in Semarang (especially _mangga arumanis_).
    

---

### 4. 🍈 **Papaya (Pepaya)**

- **Eat raw:** scoop and eat, or add lime juice.
    
- **Nutrients:** vitamin C, enzymes for digestion and clear skin.
    
- **Tip:** keeps 2–3 days in fridge once cut.
    

---

### 5. 🍏 **Guava (Jambu Biji)**

- **Eat raw:** slice thin, can sprinkle a bit of salt or chili powder.
    
- **Nutrients:** super high vitamin C (more than oranges).
    
- **Tip:** red-fleshed jambu is sweeter, softer to eat.
    

---

## 🌿 **Bonus: Super Easy Local Combos**

You can make these without “real cooking”:

- **Lalapan set:** cucumber + long bean + tomato + sambal (raw/boiled 1 min).
    
- **Salad simple:** grated carrot + tomato + lime juice.
    
- **Quick soup:** boil water, add spinach + carrot slices + a little salt (3 min).
    
- **Snack plate:** papaya + banana + watermelon chunks.
    

---

## 💡 **Tips for Lazy but Healthy Eating**

- Buy, wash, and prep **once a week** → store in containers.
    
- Use **boiling water kettle** for quick blanching (no full cooking needed).
    
- Avoid adding too much sugar or fried dressings.
    
- Drink water or fresh coconut water to stay hydrated -- helps skin glow.
