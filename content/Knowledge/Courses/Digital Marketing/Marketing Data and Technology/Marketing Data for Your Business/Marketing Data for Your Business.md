# Marketing Data for Your Business

## Leveraging Marketing Data

**[[Lead Generation Company]]** - a business whose revenue model relies on identifying and producing potential customers in a given industry or vertical

**[[Average Order Value]]** - the average dollar amount a customer spends each time they place an online order

**[[Customer lifetime value]]** - the total worth of a customer throughout their entire relationship with a business is the calculation of the profit of all future interactions with a customer which means that it could be applied to an eCommerce business as well

**[[Clickthrough Rate]]** - to see how relevant your pages and ads are to users.

**[[Track conversion rates]]** - for how often users are taking meaningful actions to your business.

**[[Metrics]]** - baseline data and make changes to your pages, posts, and ads, Used as ways to measure qualitative data about the customer experience.

**[[Return on Ad Spend|ROAS]]** - Return on Ad Spend is a metric calculated by dividing the total revenue generated by a campaign by the total amount spent on advertising for that campaign. Essentially, it measures the return on investment specifically from your advertising efforts

**[[Return on Investment|ROI]]** - Return on Investment is a metric calculated by dividing the net profit by the total cost of the investment. It measures the efficiency and profitability, expressing the return as a percentage.

**[[Brand Awareness]]** - is measured in multiple ways such as impressions, social media mentions, and google trends to name a few.

## Best Metrics

#### **Core Concepts**

* **Conversion Metrics**: Measure effectiveness of marketing efforts in converting visitors into leads/customers.
* **Conversion Examples**: Newsletter signups, purchases, downloads, bookings, registrations.
* **Purpose**: Understand valuable user actions → optimize strategies and investments.

---

#### **Marketing Path Variability**

* Different marketing channels = different user paths = different metrics.

  * *Example*: Google Ads funnel ≠ Email marketing funnel.

---

#### **Digital Marketing Flow**

1. **Ad Placement**

   * On websites, apps, connected TV, maps, shopping platforms, search.
2. **Impression**

   * Ad shown on a device screen.
   * *Does not mean the user saw it*.
3. **Click**

   * User interacts by clicking the ad.
   * Indicates interest.

---

#### **Clickthrough Rate (CTR)**

* **Formula**: (Clicks ÷ Impressions) × 100
* **Purpose**: Measures ad relevance and audience engagement.
* **Applications**: Paid ads, websites, email, social media, eCommerce.

---

#### **Tracking User Actions (Conversions)**

* **Examples**:

  * Lead form submissions
  * Appointment bookings
  * Webinar registrations
  * Purchase orders
* **Key Tools**: Google Ads, Analytics, CRM platforms, Email services.
* **Metrics to Monitor**:

  * Total conversions
  * Conversion rate
  * Cost per conversion

---

#### **Email Marketing Metrics**

* **Delivered Rate**: Emails successfully delivered.
* **Open Rate**: Emails opened.
* **Click Rate**: Clicks on links within emails.
* **Unsubscribe Rate**: Recipients opting out.

**CPI – [[Cost Per Install]]**

* Measures how much it costs to get one user to install your mobile app.
* Formula: **Total Ad Spend ÷ Number of Installs**
* Example: If you spend \$500 and get 250 installs → **\$2 CPI**

---

**CPC – [[Cost Per Click]]**

* Measures the cost for each click on your ad.
* Formula: **Total Ad Spend ÷ Number of Clicks**
* Example: If you spend \$100 and get 50 clicks → **\$2 CPC**

---

**CPM – [[Cost Per Mille]] (1,000 Impressions)**

* Measures how much you pay to show your ad 1,000 times.
* Formula: **(Total Ad Spend ÷ Total Impressions) × 1,000**
* Example: \$200 spent on 50,000 impressions → **\$4 CPM**

---

**CPV – [[Cost Per View]]**

* Measures the cost for each video view (mainly in video ads).
* Formula: **Total Ad Spend ÷ Number of Views**
* Example: \$150 spent on 1,000 views → **\$0.15 CPV**
