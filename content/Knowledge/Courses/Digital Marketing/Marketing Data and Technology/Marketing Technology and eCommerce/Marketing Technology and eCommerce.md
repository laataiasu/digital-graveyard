# Marketing Technology and eCommerce

## Technology Landscape

### **Technology Landscape Changes (Past Year & 2020s Trends)**

#### **Connectivity & Data Growth**

* Most devices (smart TVs, tablets, phones) are now internet-connected.
* Increased device usage = more data + larger file sizes.
* Cloud software/apps are standard; data is stored remotely rather than on physical devices.

#### **Rise of AI, Automation, and Machine Learning**

* Essential for tracking, managing, and analyzing massive datasets.
* Automation is widespread across industries:

  * Client outreach via automated emails, texts, DMs.
  * Follow-ups and conversations are handled 24/7 by bots.
* Surge in demand for tech-related roles (e.g., data scientists).
* AI tools handle repetitive marketing and sales tasks efficiently.

#### **Remote Work & Marketing Evolution**

* Remote work and video conferencing (Zoom, Teams) became mainstream.
* New laws/regulations forced businesses to shift from in-person to digital strategies.
* Marketing focus shifted from pricing to **“convenience”** as a competitive edge.
* Integrated technology ecosystems are becoming common.

## Marketing Technology for Your Business

**Marketing Technology Tools – Structured Learning Summary**

---

### 📊 **Categories of Marketing Tech Tools**

---

#### **1. Online Advertising Platforms**

* Used to run paid ads on search & social platforms
* Types of ads:

  * **SEM ads** – Triggered by searcher behavior/keywords
  * **Social media ads** – Based on consumer data & user engagement
* Include features:

  * Tagging
  * Remarketing
* Examples:

  * **Google Ads**
  * **Facebook Ads**
  * **Bing Ads**
  * **TikTok Ads**
  * **LinkedIn Ads**
  * **Yahoo Gemini Ads**
* Tool:

  * **Tag Assistant Legacy** – Google plugin to verify ad tags on webpages
* Note: Ads cost money; platform setup is often free

---

#### **2. Tracking Tools**

* Purpose: Tag, track, analyze, visualize user data
* Price Range: Free to \$\$\$\$ (e.g., Hyros at \$2,000+/month)
* Examples:

  * **Google Analytics** – Website performance tracking
  * **Google Data Studio** – Visualize and report data
  * **Hyros** – Advanced multi-channel sales attribution and tracking
* Industry Trends:

  * Shift toward **cookieless** tracking (due to iOS14 privacy updates)
  * Decline in reliance on device identifiers (IDFAs)
  * Emphasis on **first-party data** (owned customer data)

---

#### **3. CRM Tools (Customer Relationship Management)**

* Functions:

  * Create landing pages, forms, funnels
  * Manage lead generation
  * Email & SMS nurturing
  * Automated workflows
* Benefits:

  * Stay in touch with both new and existing customers
  * Essential for long-term customer relationships
* Examples:

  * **GoHighLevel**
  * **Hubspot**
  * **Salesforce**
  * **MailChimp** – Email automation
  * **Twilio** – Text (SMS) marketing

---

#### **4. eCommerce Tools**

* Enable businesses to sell products/services online
* Critical as online shopping surpasses in-store sales
* Examples:

  * **Shopify** – Build and manage online stores
  * **Samcart** – Sales funnel system with checkout templates and tracking
  * **Stripe** – Online payment processing and global payouts

## eCommerce

**eCommerce Marketing & Strategy – Structured Summary for Learning**

---

### 💻 **eCommerce Basics**

* **eCommerce** = Buying/selling goods & services online
* Business models:

  * Sell **products** via your website using **Stripe** for payments and delivery services for fulfillment
  * Sell **services** online via **automated booking tools** like **Calendly**, then fulfill service in-person or remotely

---

### 📈 **eCommerce Growth & Benefits**

* U.S. consumers spent **\$933B+ in 2021** on eCommerce
* Accelerated adoption post-2020
* Expanded features:

  * Multiple **payment options**
  * Integration with tools like:

    * **Inventory management systems**
    * **Chatbots**
* Mainstream rise of **contactless service**
* Faster collection of:

  * **Customer reviews**
  * **Service feedback**
* Better **customer understanding** and experience

---

## eCommerce

**🧭 Example User Path to Purchase (eCommerce Funnel)**

1. **Ad Click**

   * Platform: Google / Facebook / TikTok ad
   * Goal: Capture user interest and drive traffic to website

2. **Landing Page**

   * Purpose: Introduce offer or product line
   * Encourages next action (e.g., "Shop Now")

3. **Product Page**

   * Example: Phone Case product details
   * Includes: Photos, descriptions, reviews, "Add to Cart" button

4. **Shopping Cart**

   * Shows selected items
   * Option to edit cart or proceed to checkout

5. **Billing & Shipping Information Page**

   * User enters:

     * Name
     * Address
     * Payment info
     * Shipping preference

6. **Review Order Page**

   * Confirm:

     * Items
     * Prices
     * Total costs
     * Shipping method

7. **Order Confirmation Page**

   * Purchase completed
   * Displays:

     * Confirmation message
     * Order summary
     * Estimated delivery time

---

**Note**: Steps can vary (more or fewer), but always lead to a **final conversion (sale)**.

### 🔧 **Common Reasons to Use Marketing Tech & eCommerce Tools**

* **Connect with new users online**
  (e.g., digital ads, SEO, social media outreach)
