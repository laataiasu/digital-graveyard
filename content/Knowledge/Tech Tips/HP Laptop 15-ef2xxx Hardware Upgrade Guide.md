# HP Laptop 15-ef2xxx Hardware Upgrade Guide

- **CPU**: AMD Ryzen 5 5500U (6 cores, 12 threads, up to 4.0 GHz)
- **Form Factor**: 15.6" laptop (HP Laptop 15-ef2xxx series)

---

## Max RAM Capacity & Current Configuration

- **Maximum Supported Capacity**: 32 GB (2 × 16 GB modules)
- **Total Slots**: 2 SODIMM slots
- **Current Installed RAM** (12 GB total):
    - Slot 1: 8 GB DDR4-3200 SODIMM
    - Slot 2: 4 GB DDR4-3200 SODIMM

### Upgrade Options

- **To 16 GB**: Replace the 4 GB stick with an 8 GB DDR4-3200 SODIMM (giving 8 GB + 8 GB = 16 GB dual-channel).
- **To 32 GB (Max recommended)**: Replace both sticks with a 32 GB kit (2 × 16 GB DDR4-3200 SODIMM).

---

## Search Keywords to Buy RAM

When shopping on Amazon, Tokopedia, Shopee, Newegg, or any retailer, search for:

### Exact Specification Search

```
DDR4 SODIMM 3200MHz 16GB
```

or for a dual kit:

```
DDR4 SODIMM 3200MHz 32GB kit 2x16GB
```

### Key Specs to Double-Check

- **Form Factor**: SODIMM (laptop RAM, 260-pin — not standard desktop DIMM)
- **Type**: DDR4
- **Speed / Frequency**: 3200 MHz (also listed as PC4-25600 / 3200 MT/s)
- **Voltage**: 1.2V (Non-ECC, Unbuffered)

> [!tip] For best compatibility, match the existing module's part number when upgrading one stick. If replacing both, a matched kit ensures dual-channel performance.
