# Gulf Tech Job Hiring Windows

For tech roles in the Gulf (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar):

- **September–November** is historically one of the strongest hiring windows — budgets and headcount approvals unlock after the summer slowdown; August is best used as prep month (CV, LinkedIn, company mapping, recruiter outreach).
- **January–February** is the other peak, aligned with new-year budget cycles.
- **Don't fixate on Dubai.** Abu Dhabi plus Saudi (Vision 2030 giga-projects, banking/fintech) and Qatar (energy, finance) are often hotter markets for data roles.
- High-demand sectors: tech/digital, finance, energy, engineering & construction, healthcare, professional services, hospitality (pre-peak-season).
- Caveat: applies mainly to corporate/permanent white-collar roles; contract and oil & gas rotational roles hire year-round.
